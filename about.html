Our history
01 / How it started

From general trade
to greater possibility.

Reevbert Company Ltd began in 2017 as a general trading and supply business. We started small, paid attention to what people needed, and earned our place by delivering quality service consistently.

In 2021, we expanded into micro-credit as Reevbert Micro-Credit Enterprise, registered by the Bank of Ghana and under the Micro-Credit Association of Ghana.

Today, we are strengthening our trading and supply work while actively exploring logistics and commodity trading. The ambition is simple: to build a lasting presence in Ghana and across West Africa, creating mutual value with every partner.

05 / Key management

Steady leadership.
Wide experience.

Hon. Albert Kpakpo Mensah has been managing Reevbert since 2017, after several years of working in both local and multinational companies. His career spans insurance, outsourcing, manufacturing, logistics and agriculture, giving him a practical and grounded understanding of business growth and financial discipline.

He previously served as Head of Finance at Cocoa Inputs Limited and has led finance teams at Koudijs Ghana Ltd. Before joining Koudijs, he worked with Movis Ghana Ltd and Raanan Fish Feed. Earlier in his career, he built a strong foundation in insurance and financial operations at Enterprise Life Assurance Company and E-Services Africa Ltd., rising through the ranks from premium administration to finance roles.

Albert graduated from Cape Coast Polytechnic in 2002 with an HND in Accounting, earned a BSc in Accounting from Central University College in 2007, and completed his MBA in Finance at the University of Cape Coast in 2022.
