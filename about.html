Our history
01 / How it started

From general trade
to greater possibility.

Reevbert Enterprise began in 2017 as a general trading and supply business. We started small, paid attention to what people needed, and earned our place by delivering quality service consistently.

In 2021, we expanded into micro-credit as Reevbert Micro-Credit Enterprise, registered by the Bank of Ghana and under the Micro-Credit Association of Ghana.

Today, we are strengthening our trading and supply work while actively exploring logistics and commodity trading. The ambition is simple: to build a lasting presence in Ghana and across West Africa, creating mutual value with every partner.
