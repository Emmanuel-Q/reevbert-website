Trading & supply

Reliable sourcing & timely supply

We connect buyers with trusted suppliers, managing end-to-end procurement so customers receive the right product, at the right time and at a fair price. Our trading work covers local and regional sourcing, quality inspection, packaging and coordinated delivery. We also handle import documentation and partner with reliable carriers to reduce lead times and minimise risk.

For businesses that rely on consistent stock levels, Reevbert offers tailored supply plans, flexible ordering and responsive customer service to keep operations moving.

Micro-credit

Practical financial support

Reevbert Micro-Credit provides quick, respectful loans for individuals, employees, small businesses and churches. Applications are straightforward, decisions are fast, and our team explains terms clearly so borrowers understand repayments and fees.

Typical uses include meeting urgent working capital needs, managing cashflow between cycles, or financing small projects. We assess affordability responsibly and offer flexible repayment plans tied to our customers' cashflows.

Logistics & commodities

Movement, storage and commodity trading

We arrange warehousing, transport and commodity procurement across Ghana and the wider region. Our logistics services include goods handling, storage, route planning, customs and documentation support, and last-mile coordination to ensure timely delivery.

We trade essential commodity groups — from consumables and fertilizers to petroleum products — and build partnerships that keep supply chains resilient and predictable for our clients.
